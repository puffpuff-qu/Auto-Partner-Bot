## Copy for listings and profiles

### Short description

Discord advertising and partnership bot. Free server promotion, automatic ads every 4 hours, and two Premium slots for $3.99/month.

### Opening paragraph

Auto Partner Bot helps you advertise your Discord server through a partnership network organized by member count. Create your listing with `/setup`, promote it for free with `/advertise`, or enable automatic posting with `/auto advertise`. Your ad includes your server icon, description, tags, member count, and invite button.

### Premium summary

Two servers on Auto Ad for $3.99/month, about 13 cents a day. Premium slots advertise on a four-hour cooldown without requiring votes. Keep your free vote slot active too, and you can manage up to three servers. Run `/buy premium` for checkout and the final price.

## What to improve first

1. **Update both Top.gg description fields.** Use the short description above and a clear long description covering features, categories, setup, and Premium. Preview before saving. Top.gg's help guide says the short description contributes to discovery on its own site.
2. **Keep the official name consistent.** Use “Auto Partner Bot” on the listing, public repository, support server, and other official profiles. Keep the same bot ID and working invite links.
3. **Choose relevant Top.gg categories.** Use the categories its editor offers that accurately fit advertising, partnerships, community, or utility. Do not select unrelated categories for exposure.
4. **Link useful official pages together.** The public README links to Top.gg near the top. Add the listing to your official support server's bot-information channel and relevant profiles. Avoid bulk link drops or purchased backlinks.
5. **Show the product.** Add clear, current screenshots of setup, an advertisement, and Auto Ad management. Use descriptive captions and alt text. Hide private information and label examples as examples.
6. **Invite honest feedback.** Ask actual users for honest reviews after they have used the bot. Do not buy reviews or require positive ratings.
7. **Keep the details current.** Update prices, member categories, commands, and vote requirements when the bot changes. Remove old claims rather than appending conflicting new ones.

## Questions worth answering on the listing

- **Is it free?** Manual advertising is free. Voting unlocks one temporary Auto Ad slot; Premium is optional.
- **How do I advertise my Discord server?** Add the bot, run `/setup`, and then `/advertise`. The minimum server size is 15 members.
- **How often does it advertise?** Manual ads have a six-hour cooldown. Auto Ad has a four-hour cooldown while access remains active.
- **Which servers see my ad?** Eligible setup servers in the same member-count category. Available partner channels determine delivery.
- **Can large servers use it?** Yes. Separate categories cover 10,001 to 100,000, 100,001 to 500,000, and 500,001+ members.
- **What does Premium cost?** $3.99/month for two additional Auto Ad slots. Checkout shows the final price.

Answer these for readers, not to promise FAQ rich results. Do not claim guaranteed joins, unverified audience numbers, or a “number one” ranking.

## Google: what is and is not under your control

You can improve the listing's copy, usefulness, accuracy, and links from official pages. Top.gg controls its page title, canonical URL, robots rules, sitemap, and technical rendering. A file in this repository cannot change those settings or force Google to index or rank that page.

If you later launch an official website on a domain you control, verify it in Google Search Console. Use that site's data to see which queries bring impressions and clicks, then improve pages that answer those queries. Search Console access for your domain does not give you access to Top.gg's search data.

Rankings and traffic are not guaranteed. Google's guidance favors original, useful information over content written mainly to manipulate rankings.
