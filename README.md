## Less partnership work for your staff

Create your listing once. Auto Partner Bot shares it in eligible servers' partnership channels, and your chosen channel receives ads from the network.

| Reach your size range | Keep your listing active | Keep control in Discord |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Advertise in your size range

**A 50,000-member community shares an ad pool with servers between 10,001 and 100,000 members.** A 150,000-member community advertises in the 100,001 to 500,000 category.

Ads are shared only between eligible servers in the same category:

| Example server size | Advertises with servers of |
| :--- | :--- |
| 200 members | 15 to 250 members |
| 1,500 members | 251 to 3,000 members |
| 7,500 members | 3,001 to 10,000 members |
| **50,000 members** | **10,001 to 100,000 members** |
| **150,000 members** | **100,001 to 500,000 members** |
| **600,000 members** | **500,001+ members** |

Categories use the server's current member count when available. Delivery depends on eligible partner channels in your category.

## Give people a reason to join

Your listing brings your **server icon, description, tags, member count, and invite button** together in one ad. Tell people what happens in your community and who it is for. Animated server icons are supported.

Pick an existing partnership channel or let the bot create one during setup. Optional reminder channels and roles help your members know when manual advertising is ready.

## Three steps to your first ad

1. **[Add Auto Partner Bot](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1517257160273297562&permissions=8&scope=bot%20applications.commands).** Your server needs at least 15 members.
2. **Run `/setup`.** Choose your channel and server type, then add your description and tags. The bot creates the invite.
3. **Run `/advertise`.** Your listing is sent to eligible partner channels in your size category. Enable Auto Ad when you want scheduled posting.

The invite requests Administrator permission so the bot can configure and use its channels. [Talk to us](https://discord.gg/dmnw66HHmK) if you want help with setup.
